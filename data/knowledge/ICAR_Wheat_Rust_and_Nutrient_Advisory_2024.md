## Section 1: Yellow (Stripe) Rust (*Puccinia striiformis*)

### 1.1 Symptoms and Microclimate Conditions
Yellow rust is characterized by bright yellow, linear pustules (uredinia) arranged in stripes between leaf veins on foliage and leaf sheaths.
- Favorable conditions: Cool, moist weather with temperatures between 10°C and 15°C accompanied by morning dew or high relative humidity (>80%).
- High temperatures exceeding 25°C arrest pathogen development.

### 1.2 IPM Interventions
- Spray Propiconazole 25% EC @ 0.1% (1 ml/litre) or Tebuconazole 25.9% EC @ 1 ml/litre immediately upon detection of first pustules.

## Section 2: Split Fertilizer Application & Critical Stages

### 2.1 Nitrogen Scheduling (Target Yield 5.0 t/ha)
- Total Nitrogen recommendation: 120 - 150 kg N/ha.
- Basal application: 50% N (with full Phosphorus and Potash doses) at sowing.
- First top-dressing: 25% N at Crown Root Initiation (CRI) stage (20-25 days post-sowing, with first irrigation).
- Second top-dressing: 25% N at Jointing/Tillering stage (40-45 days post-sowing, with second irrigation).
- Deficiency symptoms: Uniform pale green to yellowing starting from bottom leaves moving upward.
